Email campaigns let you reach your clients directly with a message you write — a seasonal promotion, a win-back offer, a thank-you. You control the audience, write (or generate) the copy, preview how it looks, and send when you're ready.

Go to **Marketing** in the sidebar, then open the **Campaigns** tab.

## Starting a campaign

Click **New campaign**. You'll land on a template picker with four pre-built starting points:

| Template | Best for |
|---|---|
| **We Miss You** | Clients you haven't seen in a while |
| **Leave Us a Review** | Clients who just visited — asks for a Google or Yelp review |
| **Win-Back 10% Off** | Lapsed clients — includes a spot for a discount code |
| **Happy Birthday** | Birthday message with a booking link |

Click any template to load it into the editor with the subject line and body pre-filled. Choose **Start blank** if you'd rather write from scratch.

## The campaign editor

The editor is split into two panels: **compose** on the left, **preview** on the right. The preview updates live as you type.

![Campaign editor showing the compose panel on the left and live email preview on the right](/help-assets/screenshots/marketing/campaign-editor.png)

### Audience

The **Audience** dropdown controls who receives the campaign:

- **All clients** — every opted-in client in your list with a valid email address
- **Clients who haven't visited in N days** — enter the number of days; clients whose last completed visit was longer ago than that threshold are included
- **Clients with no visits yet** — clients who have never completed an appointment (useful for new-client follow-ups)

The audience size shown below the dropdown is an estimate based on opted-in clients with email addresses. The final count is calculated at send time — clients who unsubscribed or lack an email are excluded automatically.

### Subject line

Enter the subject line your clients will see in their inbox. A clear, direct subject generally outperforms a vague one.

### Email body

Type directly in the body field or use the toolbar buttons:

- **AI draft** — generates a warm, concise email body based on your audience and any promo code you've selected. Click it at any point to replace the body with an AI-written draft. You can edit the result freely afterward.
- **Booking link** — inserts a placeholder that becomes a branded "Book Your Appointment →" button in the sent email, linking to your public booking page.
- **Promo code picker** — select one of your active promo codes to attach it to the campaign. Once selected, click the prompt that appears to insert the code placeholder into the body at your cursor position. The promo code renders as a styled coupon block in the sent email.

The body supports plain HTML if you want finer formatting control.

### Live preview

The right panel shows the email exactly as a client named "Jane Smith" would receive it — placeholder text is substituted, the booking button renders, and the promo code block appears if you've included one. On mobile, tap **Preview** to toggle between the compose and preview panels.

## Saving a draft

Click **Save draft** at any time to save without sending. Drafts appear in the Campaigns list and can be reopened and edited later.

## Sending

Click **Send now** when you're ready. A confirmation dialog shows the subject line and asks you to confirm — campaigns cannot be recalled after sending. Once confirmed, the campaign is delivered immediately to everyone in your audience segment.

After sending, the campaign moves to the **History** tab where you can track open and click rates. See [Tracking Opens & Clicks](/help/marketing/tracking-opens-and-clicks).

## What clients see

Clients receive the email from your shop's configured sending address. The email includes your shop name in the header and an unsubscribe notice at the bottom. Clients who reply "unsubscribe" or click any unsubscribe link are opted out and will not receive future campaigns.
