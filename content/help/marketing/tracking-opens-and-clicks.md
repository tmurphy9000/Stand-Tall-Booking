After a campaign is sent, Stand Tall Booking tracks how many recipients opened the email and how many clicked a link inside it. These stats appear in the **History** tab under **Marketing**.

![Campaign send history showing campaigns with open rates and click rates](/help-assets/screenshots/marketing/campaign-analytics.png)

## Reading the history table

Each row in the history table represents one campaign send. The columns are:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Campaign** | The campaign name and the date and time it was sent |
| **Segment** | The audience the campaign was sent to (e.g., "All clients", "Clients who haven't visited in N days") |
| **Sent** | The number of recipients the campaign was delivered to |
| **Opens** | The open rate as a percentage, with the raw count in parentheses |
| **Clicks** | The click rate as a percentage, with the raw count in parentheses |

## How opens are measured

An open is recorded when a recipient's email client loads the images in the email. This works automatically — there's nothing for the recipient to do.

**Important:** Many email clients (including Apple Mail with Mail Privacy Protection, and most corporate email systems) block image loading by default. This means some opens are not counted even when the client did read the email. The open rate shown is a floor, not the true total — actual engagement is almost always higher.

## How clicks are measured

A click is recorded when a recipient clicks any link inside the email — including the booking button and any review links. Click tracking is more reliable than open tracking because it does not depend on image loading.

## Interpreting your rates

Industry benchmarks for small local businesses typically run:

- Open rates: 25–45%
- Click rates: 3–10%

Because open tracking undercounts, a 20% open rate may reflect 35–40% actual opens. Focus on clicks as the more reliable signal of engagement, and on bookings that follow a campaign as the ultimate outcome to track.

## Empty history

The History tab shows "No send history yet" until at least one campaign has been sent. Drafts and templates do not appear here — only campaigns that have been delivered to recipients.
